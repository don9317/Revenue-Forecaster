Future Month Adjustment Planner

Review the system forecast for upcoming months and add management knowledge the historical model cannot see. Adjustments are independent by month and revenue type.

How to read this forecast: Booked revenue comes from future reservations already present in the MSS Reservation CSV. System Forecast applies the dashboard's forecasting logic. Management Adjustments are the month-specific amounts you enter. Final Forecast combines the system forecast and those adjustments. Future months are never labeled as actual revenue.
