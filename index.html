Forecast Scenario iForecast Scenario
Applies a broad growth assumption to the system forecast. It does not change actual or historical revenue. Specific future revenue known by management should be entered separately in the Future Month Adjustment Planner.

Select the broad forecasting assumption. The dollar effect is shown in the Selected-Month Forecast Baseline & Growth section below.

Scenario vs. management entries: Scenario = broad modeling assumption. Future Month Planner = specific revenue the facility knows about. Actual and historical reports are never changed by this selector.

Overall Outlook Assumption iMonth-specific broad assumption
Use this for economic, industry or local-market growth/decline—not for known bookings. Enter the percentage for each month in the consolidated table below.

Apply a broad economic/industry/local-market adjustment month by month in the Outlook % column below. Known rentals/events belong on Rentals & Events; membership assumptions belong on Memberships. Historical reports are never changed.

Consolidated Monthly Forecast

Review and finalize—do not re-enter category forecasts here. Rentals & Events and Membership forecasts flow here automatically. Set a month-specific Overall Outlook % and, only when needed, one Final Management Adjustment with a reason.

Workflow: Stream forecasts → Overall Outlook % → Final Management Adjustment = Final Forecast. The Final Forecast feeds the dashed lines on Overview.

Selected Forecast Month — System Forecast Calculation iRead-only calculation
This is an output, not a data-entry area. Baseline is the system forecast before growth assumptions. Growth is the dollar effect of the selected Forecast Scenario and selected-month Growth Adjustment Inputs. The large number is the resulting system forecast.

Read-only. Baseline + Growth = System Forecast for the selected Forecast Month. Change the Forecast Scenario or Growth Adjustment Inputs to change these numbers.
